Sync

No calibration yet. Press Calibrate with microphone; the trims in force are on each speaker card.

One chirp per speaker, recorded with the laptop microphone; everyone is then delayed to match the slowest speaker. Sound takes about 3 ms per metre to reach the microphone, so enter each speaker's distance from the laptop on its card: that travel time is taken out and the speakers themselves are aligned, wherever the laptop stands. Without distances the sound is aligned where the laptop is. Keep the room quiet.
