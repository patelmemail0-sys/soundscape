Sync

Plays one chirp per speaker, three seconds apart, records them with the microphone and delays everyone to match the slowest speaker. Keep the room quiet; put the laptop roughly between the speakers. Never use a Bluetooth speaker's own microphone: it switches that speaker into headset mode and breaks the timing.

No calibration yet. Current trims are shown on each speaker card.

Add a speaker

Put the speaker in pairing mode (hold its Bluetooth button), scan, then add it with a position: left and right are special for stereo split, anything else is a name.
